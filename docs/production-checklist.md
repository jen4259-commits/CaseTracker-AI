# Pilot 1 Production Checklist

## Intake and research
- [ ] Case selected and selection rationale recorded
- [ ] Procedural posture and last-checked date recorded
- [ ] Research scope and limitations documented
- [ ] Source records captured with attribution and retrieval details

## Verification
- [ ] Material claims listed and linked to source/evidence locators
- [ ] Allegations distinguished from findings and established facts
- [ ] Conflicts and uncertainty reviewed and documented
- [ ] Time-sensitive facts checked close to publication
- [ ] Human fact-check approval recorded

## Story and production
- [ ] Timeline and episode outline reviewed
- [ ] Script and on-screen claims linked to claim/source records
- [ ] Media asset provenance and context checked
- [ ] Rights, permissions, and attribution recorded for each asset
- [ ] Narration, host intro/outro, overlays, and audio mix reviewed
- [ ] Edit timings and final runtime checked

## Final review and export
- [ ] Final cut checked against sources
- [ ] Names, captions, dates, graphics, and clip context checked
- [ ] Privacy and legal/ethical review completed
- [ ] Brand and audio quality review completed
- [ ] Required human approvals recorded
- [ ] Master and platform versions exported and playback-checked
- [ ] Checksums, locations, and export metadata recorded
- [ ] Traceability report and archive package complete
- [ ] No unresolved publication-blocking issue remains
