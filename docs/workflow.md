# Pilot 1 Workflow

## 1. Intake
- Create a case record and capture the proposed story angle and reason for selection.
- Record jurisdiction, procedural posture, important dates, and open questions.
- Keep leads separate from verified facts.

## 2. Research and source capture
- Collect official records, attributable reporting, statements, and relevant media.
- Record source URLs/citations, title, author or issuing body, dates, retrieval date, and access notes.
- Record search scope and limitations; do not claim that every possible website was searched.
- Note permissions, license, attribution, and reuse restrictions for each media asset.

## 3. Verification
- Break the story into checkable claims.
- Link each material claim to supporting evidence with a page, paragraph, timestamp, or other locator.
- Assess source reliability and whether the source is primary or secondary.
- Flag conflicting, incomplete, or time-sensitive information for human review.
- Record reviewer, date, and decision; preserve uncertainty where it remains.

## 4. Story design
- Build a dated timeline and distinguish alleged events from judicial findings and other established facts.
- Develop an outline and narration blueprint from supported claims.
- Link each script section and storyboard item to relevant claim/source IDs.
- Identify context, caveats, and updates needed before release.

## 5. Production
- Select media only after context and rights checks.
- Track asset provenance, intended segment, in/out timecodes, attribution, and any edits.
- Prepare narration, host intro/outro, overlays, stills, and audio mix.
- Keep an edit decision list or equivalent record so the final cut can be audited.

## 6. Review
- Fact-check the final cut against the current source log.
- Check clip context, dates, captions, names, on-screen claims, and audio levels.
- Review privacy, permissions, attribution, and legal/ethical concerns.
- Record explicit human approvals and unresolved issues.

## 7. Export and archive
- Export the approved master and distribution versions.
- Record format, runtime, checksum, date, and output location.
- Archive the source traceability report, script, storyboard, asset list, and review approvals.
- Do not publish if a required review gate is incomplete.
