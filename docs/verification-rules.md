# Verification Rules

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Every material factual claim in a script, graphic, or narration must link to one or more sources.
2. Record a precise locator where possible: page, paragraph, docket entry, exhibit, or media timecode.
3. Claims must be checked in context. A clip or excerpt must not be presented in a way that changes its meaning.
4. Distinguish allegations, arguments, opinions, official findings, and final outcomes.
5. Record the date checked for facts that can change, including procedural status.
6. Flag contradictions and unresolved uncertainty. Do not let an automated score erase disagreement.
7. Automated extraction or summarization is a research aid, not verification or editorial approval.
8. Check media source, authenticity indicators, date, context, edits, attribution, and rights/permissions before use.
9. Keep an audit trail of source changes, review decisions, and corrections.
10. Require human approval before publication.

## Source assessment

For each source, record who created or issued it, when it was created/published, how it was obtained, whether it is primary or secondary, its relevance, and known limitations. Prefer original records for claims about what a record says, while recognizing that a primary source may still be incomplete, disputed, or mistaken.

## Claim review outcomes

- `unreviewed`: not assessed
- `in_review`: assessment underway
- `supported`: cited material supports the claim as worded
- `partially_supported`: support exists but wording or scope needs revision
- `conflicted`: credible sources materially disagree
- `unsupported`: adequate support was not found
- `rejected`: evidence contradicts the claim or the claim is not suitable for use

## Media rights and integrity

Publicly viewable does not mean reusable. Record license/permission, attribution requirements, restrictions, and the intended use. Keep source originals where lawful and appropriate and identify any crop, edit, enhancement, or transcription. Do not use fabricated, misattributed, or context-stripped evidence.

## Sensitive information

Minimize personal data that is not necessary to the story. Apply additional care to minors, victims, witnesses, private individuals, and sealed or restricted records. Escalate sensitive decisions to a human reviewer.

## Publication gate

A production cannot be marked approved when a material claim is unsupported, an important conflict is unresolved, rights are unclear, or required review is missing. Document corrections and update affected exports if material errors are found after publication.
